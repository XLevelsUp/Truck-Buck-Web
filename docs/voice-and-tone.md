# Voice and tone

**We sound like a dependable transport manager who also happens to be good on the phone:**
clear, calm, specific, never salesy. Many readers are booking a truck online for the first time,
some on a slow phone, some reading in their second language.

_Numbers and references in the examples below are illustrative, not commitments._

## Five rules

1. **Short sentences.** One idea each. Aim for under 15 words; 20 is the limit.
2. **Numbers over adjectives.** "Fare shown before you book" beats "transparent pricing".
   "Call back within 30 minutes" beats "fast support". **Only numbers we can prove**, and none at
   all until ops confirm them.
3. **Plain words, no jargon.** Explain a trade term **once**, the first time it appears on a page:
   _"Full truck load (FTL): the whole truck carries only your goods."_ After that, "full truck" is fine.
4. **Say what happens next.** Every form, button and error tells people what comes next: "We'll call
   you on +91 98765 43210 within 30 minutes."
5. **Respect, not hype.** Drivers and transporters are professionals. No "rockstar", no "hustle",
   no pity.

## Do / don't

| Do                                                                | Don't                                        |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Book a truck like you book a cab.                                 | Revolutionising India's logistics ecosystem. |
| See your fare before you book.                                    | Seamless, transparent, hassle-free pricing.  |
| Only vetted transporters can take your load.                      | Best-in-class trusted partner network.       |
| Get price                                                         | Submit / Click here / Learn more             |
| Enter a 10-digit mobile number.                                   | Invalid input.                               |
| We couldn't calculate this route right now. Call us or try again. | Oops! Something went wrong 😅                |
| Your request is in. Reference TB-4821.                            | Thank you for your submission!               |

## Words we use

| Use                                     | Instead of                    |
| --------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| truck, lorry (on Hindi/Tamil pages)     | vehicle, asset, unit          |
| fare, price                             | quote rate, tariff (in copy)  |
| transporter, fleet owner                | vendor, supplier, partner     |
| driver                                  | captain, pilot, hero          |
| pickup, drop                            | origin, destination (in copy) |
| proof of delivery (POD), explained once | ePOD, docs                    |

## Formats

- **Money:** `₹18,500` with Indian grouping (`₹1,23,456`); no decimals for fares. Use `formatINR`.
- **Weight and length:** `9 tonnes`, `19 ft`, `346 km`. Units spelled out or as people say them.
- **Dates and times:** IST, `Tue, 24 Nov` and `2:30 pm`. Avoid ambiguous `11/12`.
- **Phone:** `+91 98765 43210`.
- **Buttons:** verb first, 1–3 words, sentence case: _Get price, Request a callback, Apply to join,
  Get the app._
- **Headings:** sentence case, no full stop. Questions are fine in FAQs.

## Errors and empty states

Say **what happened**, **why** if it's useful, and **what to do**, in that order. Never blame the
user. Put errors next to the field, in words, not only in red.

> Enter a 10-digit mobile number, like 98765 43210.

## Writing for translation (Hindi, Tamil)

- No idioms, puns or wordplay that only works in English.
- Never build sentences by joining strings. Use whole sentences with placeholders:
  `"We'll call you on {phone}"`.
- Leave room: Tamil runs about 40% longer than English.
- "Cab" works as-is in Hindi and Tamil. Keep the analogy.
